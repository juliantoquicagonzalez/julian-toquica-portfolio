About

For this animated short, I modeled and textured the two lead characters — the full-body, detail and turntable renders here are all pure 3D work. Beyond that, an AI exploration phase quickly put them in context: facial expressions, staging, how they'd look together. A project where 3D craft and AI tools combined to imagine the world these characters live in.
