About

A team project for Bombillo Amarillo. My role was lead 3D modeling and texturing on Bubbles and Friends — the five characters shown here were modeled and textured by me entirely. The show's stylized look runs on a very low poly count: it's built for YouTube on tight delivery schedules, so heavy meshes were never an option.
