Bubbles and Friends — Sets

Five sets built for Bubbles and Friends, a stylized animated series for YouTube: the Barn, Penny's House, The Learning Experience, the Fire Station, and the Treehouse.

About

A team project for Bombillo Amarillo. My role was lead 3D modeling and texturing on Bubbles and Friends — the five sets shown here were modeled and textured by me entirely. The show's stylized look runs on a very low poly count: it's built for YouTube on tight delivery schedules, so heavy meshes were never an option.
