Role clarification: the aircraft's 3D model was not built by me. My role here is 3D + AI compositor — lighting, rendering, and integrating the existing model, plus building the supporting scenes from scratch.

01 · Aircraft integration

3D stays 3D. AI only builds the world around it.

The aircraft is always kept as an unaltered 3D render — AI tends to distort shapes and logos, which isn't acceptable on a real product. AI is used exclusively for the environment surrounding it.
