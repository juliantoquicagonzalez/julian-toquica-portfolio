3D + AI · Product

About

Natilus is an aviation company innovating in a fast-moving space. As a 3D artist and AI designer, I worked on this project for Global Grid Agency: I was in charge of texturing the aircraft and integrating it into 3D and AI-generated environments, combining 3D renders, AI-built environments and Photoshop compositions to keep full control over the details, rather than leaving results to chance.

Software

Blender Substance Hunyuan 3D Weave Photoshop

Context

Client: Natilus · Via Global Grid Agency

Role clarification: the aircraft's 3D model was not built by me — my part was texturing it, then integrating it into AI-built environments and compositing the final images.

01 · Futuristic Airport

A futuristic airport, composed in Photoshop — the planes and some of the vehicles are 3D renders, layered together with other AI-generated elements. Building it this way, rather than leaving it fully to AI, kept control over exactly what appears in the scene.

02 · Hangar

A hangar scene, created entirely in 3D: I used Hunyuan 3D to quickly generate some of the basic models that helped me set up the scene, so I could focus on the composition instead of modeling everything from scratch. Created in Blender and Substance, with post-production in Photoshop.

Clay pass to final scene — pieces falling into place assets/img/natilus/hangar-assembly.mp4

03 · Aircraft integration

Each one combines a 3D render of the aircraft — accurate in shape, scale and texture — with an AI-generated environment and a Photoshop compositing pass, keeping full control over the final image.
